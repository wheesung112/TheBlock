Import the appropriate content pack (Built-in Renderer, URP or HDRP) by double clicking the package and hitting the Import button.

Use HDRP 14 package for HDRPv14 (Unity 2022.3)
Use HDRP 15 package for HDRPv15 (Unity 2023.1)
